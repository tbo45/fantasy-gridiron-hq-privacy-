Fantasy Gridiron HQ Privacy Policy
Last updated: November 14, 2026

Fantasy Gridiron HQ ("FGHQ") is a free Chrome extension that provides a consolidated live dashboard for supported Sleeper and ESPN fantasy football leagues, matchups, rosters, player performance, projections, injuries, and scoring events.

FGHQ is designed to minimize data collection. FGHQ does not operate a server that receives or stores users' fantasy account credentials.

Information FGHQ Handles
Sleeper
FGHQ uses the Sleeper username entered by the user to request read-only fantasy football information from Sleeper.

This may include:

Sleeper username and user identifier
League identifiers and league names
Fantasy team and roster information
Matchups
Player information
Fantasy scoring information
Player projections
NFL week and game-state information
FGHQ does not request or store a Sleeper password.

Sleeper information is retrieved directly from Sleeper's services for the purpose of providing FGHQ's fantasy football dashboard.

ESPN
FGHQ supports private ESPN Fantasy Football leagues using the user's existing signed-in ESPN browser session.

To authenticate requests for the user's configured ESPN fantasy leagues, FGHQ accesses ESPN authentication information associated with the browser session, including the ESPN SWID and espn_s2 cookies.

FGHQ:

Does not request the user's ESPN password
Does not display ESPN authentication cookie values
Does not include ESPN authentication information in diagnostic logs
Does not sell ESPN authentication information
Does not send ESPN authentication information to FGHQ-operated servers
ESPN authentication information is used locally by the extension to authenticate requests to ESPN for fantasy league information requested by the user.

Fantasy information retrieved from ESPN may include:

League identifiers and league names
Fantasy team identifiers and team names
Rosters
Matchups
Player information
Scores
Player and team projections
League settings
Schedule and game-state information
ESPN Tab Scanning
FGHQ includes a user-initiated feature called Scan ESPN Tabs.

FGHQ performs this scan only when the user chooses that feature.

The scan examines open browser tab URLs to locate ESPN Fantasy Football pages and may inspect the selected ESPN My Team page to identify the league and fantasy team the user wants to add.

FGHQ does not collect or store page content from unrelated websites.

Non-ESPN tabs are ignored.

Local Storage
FGHQ stores information locally in Chrome so that the extension can remember the user's configuration and operate between sessions.

Locally stored information may include:

Sleeper username
Fantasy league identifiers
Fantasy team identifiers and team names
Enabled league selections
Display and interface preferences
Matchup ordering
Scoring baselines
Cached player information
Limited diagnostic information used to identify refresh or connection errors
This information is used to provide the extension's functionality.

Diagnostics
FGHQ may store a limited number of diagnostic entries locally in Chrome when a fantasy platform refresh or connection attempt fails.

Diagnostics may include:

Platform name
Processing stage
Error message
Timestamp
Diagnostics are stored locally and are not transmitted to an FGHQ-operated analytics or diagnostic server.

Payments and Support FGHQ
Fantasy Gridiron HQ is free to use.

Users may optionally support continued development through the Fantasy Gridiron HQ Venmo Business Profile.

Contributions:

Are completely voluntary
Do not unlock features
Do not provide premium functionality
Do not change support priority
Do not affect access to FGHQ
Are not charitable donations
Are not tax deductible
Payment processing is handled externally by Venmo.

FGHQ does not receive or store:

Payment card numbers
Bank account credentials
Venmo passwords or login credentials
Sale of User Data
FGHQ does not sell user data.

FGHQ does not use fantasy account information, browser-session authentication information, or browsing information for advertising, behavioral profiling, or creditworthiness purposes.

Sharing of Information
FGHQ sends information only where necessary to provide its user-facing functionality.

For example:

Sleeper usernames and identifiers may be sent to Sleeper to retrieve requested Sleeper fantasy information.
ESPN authentication information is used in requests to ESPN to retrieve the user's requested ESPN fantasy information.
Support payments are handled directly by Venmo.
FGHQ does not operate a backend service that receives ESPN authentication information.

Third-Party Services
FGHQ interacts with third-party services including Sleeper, ESPN, and Venmo.

Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

Fantasy Gridiron HQ is an independent application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or associated with ESPN, Sleeper, the National Football League, Venmo, or any NFL team.

ESPN, Sleeper, NFL, NFL team names and logos, Venmo, and related trademarks belong to their respective owners.

Data Retention and Deletion
FGHQ configuration and cached information stored locally can be removed by:

Using the Reset option in FGHQ Settings, where applicable; or
Uninstalling the Fantasy Gridiron HQ Chrome extension and removing its locally stored extension data.
FGHQ does not maintain an external user-account database from which additional FGHQ-hosted personal data must be deleted.

Security
FGHQ uses HTTPS when communicating with supported external services.

FGHQ is designed to limit access to data to what is reasonably necessary to provide its fantasy football dashboard functionality.

Children
Fantasy Gridiron HQ is not directed specifically to children and does not knowingly operate a service intended to collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated if FGHQ's functionality or data practices change.

Material changes to data handling will also be reflected in appropriate in-extension disclosures and Chrome Web Store disclosures.

The "Last updated" date at the top of this policy identifies the current version.
